We're building the world's cheapest, most performant monitoring system, designed to be easy for agents to install and configure.

Superlog helps teams build self-healing systems by connecting their agents to telemetry and running automations that detect and remediate issues at machine speed.

## Telemetry

Superlog also stores your OpenTelemetry logs, traces, and metrics. You can search them, build dashboards, group exceptions into issues, and alert on them. Automations and agents read the same data. Telemetry is in early access.

- [Collect telemetry](/collect-telemetry/overview) from your code, a Collector, Vercel, AWS, or Google Cloud.
- [Make your application reliable](/reliability/checklist) with error notifications, alerts, synthetic checks, dashboards, and automations.

## Bring your own inference

Every automation picks a model from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, Mistral, or DeepSeek, and runs it in the Codex, Claude Agent SDK, or OpenCode harness. Add an API key or connect a ChatGPT subscription and runs use it. Without one, runs use the usage included in your plan. See [Models and harnesses](/automations/models).
