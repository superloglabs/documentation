Responder is a self-hostable AI incident-investigation platform. When a production alert fires, Responder automatically queries your observability tools, identifies the root cause, and delivers a structured report — complete with severity classification, evidence, timeline, and an optional pull request to fix the problem.

## How Responder Works

Responder watches your alert sources and automatically kicks off an investigation whenever something fires. Each investigation is handled by an **Agent** you configure — specifying which tools to query, which AI model to use, and how to deliver results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Link Slack, Sentry, Datadog, GitHub, and any other observability or cloud services your team uses. Responder keeps credentials encrypted per workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an Agent">
    Configure an Agent with a trigger (Sentry project, Datadog monitor, Slack channel), the context sources to query during an investigation, and your preferred AI model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alerts trigger investigations">
    When an alert fires, Responder automatically runs an investigation — querying logs, traces, metrics, and code — then produces a structured report with severity, root cause, timeline, and remediation options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results and ship fixes">
    View investigation reports in the dashboard or Slack. Approve an auto-generated pull request to deploy the fix, or follow the recommended remediation steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>
