Superlog receives logs, traces, and metrics over OTLP/HTTP, the standard OpenTelemetry protocol. Your app sends them with an ingest key. This guide takes you from an empty workspace to your first logs and traces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the OpenTelemetry setup">
    Open **Overview** in the sidebar. On a workspace with no telemetry, click **Set up OpenTelemetry**. Otherwise click **Add telemetry** and pick **OpenTelemetry**.

    If the page says **Sending telemetry is not available**, telemetry is not turned on for your workspace yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an ingest key">
    Click **Create ingest key**, then **Copy key**. Keys start with `sl_ingest_`.

    You can see the key only once. Store it in your secret manager. Superlog names the key after when you created it, for example `Created Oct 15, 14:32`. You can find and revoke it later in **Settings → Ingest keys**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Instrument your app">
    Use one of these:

    - **Let a coding agent do it.** Under **Paste this prompt into your coding agent**, copy the prompt and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or another agent in your project. The prompt includes your key. It tells the agent to add the OpenTelemetry SDK with automatic instrumentation, set `service.name` and `deployment.environment.name`, connect your logger, and read the key from the environment instead of committing it.
    - **Do it yourself.** Follow [Instrument your app](/guides/instrument-your-app) for Node.js, Python, Go, or Java.

    Either way, the SDK is configured with three environment variables. Use the origin of your workspace's region: `https://superlog.sh` for EU or `https://us.superlog.sh` for US.

    ```bash
    export OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=https://superlog.sh/api/ingest
    export OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_PROTOCOL=http/protobuf
    export OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_HEADERS=x-api-key=<your ingest key>
    ```

    The SDK adds `/v1/logs`, `/v1/traces`, and `/v1/metrics` to the endpoint.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your app">
    Start the app from a shell with the variables set, and send it a few requests.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See your data">
    Open **Logs** and **Traces** in the sidebar. Your service appears under the name you set in `service.name`. **Overview** lists each service that sends data, and **Metrics** shows its metrics.

    In **Settings → Ingest keys**, the key shows **Used just now** after your app sends data with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nothing shows up, see [No telemetry showing up](/troubleshooting/telemetry).

## Other ways to send telemetry

**Add telemetry** also connects cloud providers that need no code changes:

- **Amazon Web Services** streams CloudWatch metrics and logs through Firehose.
- **Google Cloud** has Superlog read Cloud Logging and Cloud Monitoring from a connected project.
- **Vercel** creates drains for the traces and logs of your deployments.

See [Instrument your app](/guides/instrument-your-app) for these and for sending from an existing OpenTelemetry Collector. For browser apps, see [Browser and React](/guides/browser).
