**Explore → Traces** has two pages: **Overview** for a service's health, and **Search** for individual traces.

## Traces overview

The overview summarizes requests. A request is a span that enters a service: a server or consumer span, or the root span of a trace.

Pick a **Service** or keep **All services**. When spans carry the `deployment.environment` resource attribute, an **Environment** list appears too. Choose a range of **15m**, **1h** (the default), **6h**, **24h**, or **7d**. Turn on **Live** to reload every 10 seconds.

Three tiles chart the range:

- **Requests** per minute, with errors marked.
- **Error rate** as a share of requests.
- **p95 latency**. Bars turn orange from 500 ms and red from 1 s.

Below them:

- **Operations**: the top request span names, with **Req/min**, **Errors**, and **p95**. Click one to search its traces.
- **Dependencies**: outgoing client calls by p95 latency. Each is named by `peer.service`, `server.address`, `net.peer.name`, or `db.system`, whichever the span has, and otherwise by its span name.
- **Top errors**: failing spans grouped by status message, with how many traces they hit and when they were first seen. Click one to search those traces, or **View all in Search**.

## Trace search

**Traces → Search** lists traces that started in the time range, newest first. It uses the same time picker as [Logs](/telemetry/logs#time-range).

The search bar works like the log search. Press `/` to focus it. Fields include:

- `service.name`, `status` (**Error**, **OK**, **Unset**), and `operation` (the span name). Plain text filters to that exact span name.
- `duration` with `>=` and a value such as `500ms`, `1s`, or `1m`, up to 60 minutes.
- `trace.id`. Paste a trace ID to open that trace directly.
- Any resource or span attribute seen in the range.

The sidebar has **Service**, **Status**, **Span name**, and **Trace duration** (**Over 100 ms** to **Over 5 s**), and every attribute key below them.

A trace matches when any of its spans matches the filters. Its row describes the whole trace:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Started** | When the first span started. |
| **Root span** | The span without a parent, its service, and how many services the trace crossed. |
| **Duration** | From the first span's start to the last span's end. |
| **Spans** | How many spans the trace has. |
| **Status** | **Error** if any span failed, otherwise **OK**. |

The histogram above counts matching spans by status. The list shows 50 traces per page.

## Trace inspector

Click a trace to open it in a panel beside the list. Use the arrows to move to the previous or next trace, or **Open full page** for a page of its own.

The header shows the request: method, route, and HTTP status code when the root span has them. Below it are the root service, duration, and counts of spans, services, and errors, with the **Trace ID** to copy.

### Waterfall

The **Waterfall** tab draws each span on a timeline, nested under its parent and colored by service.

- **Find a span or attribute** matches span names, services, span IDs, and attribute keys and values. Matching spans stay visible with their parents.
- **Show only errors** hides spans that didn't fail.
- **Collapse all** and **Expand all** fold the tree. Click a span's arrow to fold one branch.

The inspector opens on the first failing span, or the root span if none failed.

### Selected span

Click a span to see its name, service, kind, duration, start offset, and **Span ID**. A failing span shows its error message and stack trace.

Attributes are grouped:

- **HTTP**: `http.*`, `url.*`, and `server.*` attributes.
- **Request context**: the span's other attributes.
- **Resource attributes**: the sending process. Collapsed by default.

Hover an attribute for **Filter**, **Exclude**, and **Copy**. **Filter** and **Exclude** add it to your trace search.

**Logs in this span** lists the logs the span wrote. **Open in Logs** opens them in [Logs](/telemetry/logs).

### Logs and services

- **Logs** lists every log in the trace. Click a message to select the span that wrote it.
- **Services** lists each service with its spans, errors, and total **Time in spans**.

## Query traces from an agent

The [MCP server](/api-reference/mcp) has `query_traces` and `get_trace`. The [management API](/api-reference/introduction) has `POST /telemetry/traces/search` and `GET /telemetry/traces/{traceId}`.
