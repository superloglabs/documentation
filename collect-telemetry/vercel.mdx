Superlog creates a trace drain and a log drain on your Vercel team. Traces arrive as OpenTelemetry. Logs carry their request and trace IDs. Drains need a Pro or Enterprise team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Vercel setup">
    Open **Overview** in the sidebar, click **Add telemetry**, and pick **Vercel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Vercel">
    Click **Connect Vercel** and install the Superlog integration on your team. The drains cover the projects you share with the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the drains">
    Click **Create drains**. Superlog creates an ingest key for the drains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The source appears under **Connected** with its status and when data last arrived. **Remove** deletes the drains and revokes their key.

If Vercel refuses, check that the team is on Pro or Enterprise and that the integration has the Drains permission. Reconnect Vercel to grant it.
