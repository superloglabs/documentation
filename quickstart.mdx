This guide takes you from sign-up to a finished automation run on the hosted service.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account and workspace">
    Go to [superlog.sh](https://superlog.sh/app?signup=1) and click **Get started**. Sign up with Google, GitHub, or an email and password.

    Then create a workspace. Your automations, integrations, and members belong to the workspace. You become its admin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Open **Automations**. Under **Start from a template**, pick one that matches a task your team does by hand, for example **Triage new Sentry issues** or **Answer support questions**.

    The template fills in the trigger, instructions, and connectors. You can also click **Create automation** to start blank.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the trigger">
    Each automation needs at least one trigger. If the trigger's tool is not connected yet, its card shows **Connect**. Click it, approve access in the pop-up window, and then choose the Slack channels, Sentry projects, or Discord channels to watch.

    Templates with a **Schedule** trigger need no connection. Set the frequency and time instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a repository">
    Under **Repositories**, click **Add GitHub** and install the GitHub App on the repositories you want Superlog to use. Then click **Add repository** and pick at least one. The first repository is the agent's working directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a model">
    In the **Agent instructions** toolbar, click **Choose model** and pick a provider and model. Superlog picks a compatible harness for you.

    Runs use the usage included in your plan unless you add your own API key or ChatGPT subscription in **Settings → Models**. See [Models and harnesses](/automations/models).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and run">
    Click **Save**. The automation is active and runs the next time its trigger fires.

    To see it work right away, click **Run now**, or click **Test** and describe an event for the agent to handle. Open the run to follow the agent's work live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
