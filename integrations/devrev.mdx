---
title: "DevRev"
description: "Connect DevRev so a new support ticket starts an automation that looks into it and posts an internal comment."
---

DevRev starts automations when a new ticket arrives. The automation reads the ticket and posts its result as an internal comment.

## Connect DevRev

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a token">
    Sign in to DevRev as an admin of the organization. Open **Settings → Account → Personal access token** and create a token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In Superlog, open **Integrations** and click **Add** on DevRev. Paste the **Personal access token** and click **Connect DevRev**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Superlog registers a webhook for new tickets in your DevRev organization.

## Start automations from tickets

In the automation editor, click **Add trigger** and choose **DevRev → New ticket**. Each new ticket starts a run.

## What Superlog writes

The automation posts an internal comment on the ticket. The customer who opened the ticket does not see it. Comments are posted as the person who created the token.

DevRev is used by automations only. Findings do not read it.
