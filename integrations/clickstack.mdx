Superlog queries logs, traces, metrics, and services in ClickStack Cloud or a self-hosted ClickStack or HyperDX.

## Connect ClickStack

In **Integrations**, click **Add** on **ClickStack / HyperDX** and choose your deployment.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="ClickStack Cloud">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find the service ID">
        In ClickHouse Cloud, open your ClickStack service. Open **Connect → MCP** and copy its service ID.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect">
        In Superlog, the **MCP URL** is filled in. Enter the **ClickHouse service ID**, click **Continue with ClickHouse Cloud**, and approve access.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Self-hosted">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your key">
        In ClickStack, open **Team Settings → API Keys**. Copy your Personal API Access Key and the full MCP URL, which usually ends in `/api/mcp`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect">
        In Superlog, paste the **MCP URL** and **Personal API Access Key**. Click **Connect ClickStack / HyperDX**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How Superlog uses ClickStack

- **Investigations.** Superlog queries ClickStack for evidence while it investigates a problem.
- [Tag mode](/tag-mode) can read ClickStack when you turn it on for tag mode.

ClickStack is not a source of new problems or a trigger. To have Superlog find problems in the same data, also send it to Superlog with [OpenTelemetry](/guides/instrument-your-app).
