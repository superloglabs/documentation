title: "Grafana"

---

Superlog reads Grafana to find problems and to explain them. It never changes anything in Grafana.

## What Superlog reads

- Dashboards, alert rules, and annotations.
- Queries to your data sources, such as Prometheus, Loki, Tempo, and Pyroscope.
- OnCall and incidents.

Write, admin, and provisioning tools are turned off.

## Connect Grafana

Open **Setup → Sources**, click **Add source**, and choose **Grafana**. Or, in **Integrations**, click **Add** on Grafana. Then choose your deployment.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Grafana Cloud">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter your stack">
        Enter the **Stack URL**. Your stack name or any URL on it works, for example `acme` or `acme.grafana.net`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve access">
        Click **Continue with Grafana Cloud** and approve access. There is no token to copy.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Self-hosted">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a service account">
        In Grafana, open **Administration → Users and access → Service accounts**. Add a service account with the **Viewer** role, then add a token to it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect">
        Enter the **Grafana URL** and paste the **Service account token**. Click **Connect Grafana**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How findings use Grafana

- **Sweeps.** If none of your telemetry reaches Superlog, Superlog checks Grafana every 15 minutes for new problems. It looks at firing alerts first, then logs, traces, and metrics. See [How findings work](/findings/how-it-works#where-problems-come-from).
- **Investigations.** Superlog queries Grafana for evidence and impact while it investigates any problem.
- **Known problems.** A problem that a firing Grafana alert already covers is not raised by a sweep.

## Automations

Send Grafana alerts to a Slack channel. Then add a Slack **New message in channel** trigger on that channel. See [Slack](/integrations/slack).

## Limits

- A self-hosted Grafana URL must be reachable over public HTTPS.
- The token is stored encrypted and used only to read and query.
