Superlog reads PostHog logs and errors, and uses product usage to tell how many customers a problem affects.

## What Superlog reads

- Logs, error tracking, and session replays.
- Insights, dashboards, and events.

## Connect PostHog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PostHog page">
    Open **Setup → Sources**, click **Add source**, and choose **PostHog**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Click **Continue to PostHog** and approve access. The connection is read-only. Nothing in PostHog can be changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose projects">
    Back in Superlog, under **Telemetry from**, click **Add project** and choose the projects whose error logs Superlog reads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Integrations**, **Add** on PostHog asks for the access automations get:

| Access | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Read only** | Insights, dashboards, events, logs, errors, and replays. Nothing can be changed. |
| **Read and write** | Automations can also create and change insights, dashboards, alerts, feature flags, annotations, cohorts, experiments, notebooks, and surveys. Investigations, tag mode, and scans still only read. |

## How findings use PostHog

- **Error logs.** Every minute, Superlog reads the `error` and `fatal` log lines of the projects you chose. They become error issues, and Superlog investigates new and regressed ones. Lower levels stay in PostHog for investigations to read.
- **Sweeps.** If you chose no project, or your projects send no logs, and no other telemetry reaches Superlog, Superlog sweeps PostHog error tracking and logs every 15 minutes. See [How findings work](/findings/how-it-works#where-problems-come-from).
- **Investigations.** Superlog reads events and replays to count the customers a problem affects.

## Automations

Send PostHog alerts to a Slack channel. Then add a Slack **New message in channel** trigger on that channel. Resolved and recovered notifications are ignored.

## Limits

- A chosen project counts as sending while it has any log line in the last 10 minutes.
- Error logs need PostHog logs. Error tracking alone is read only by sweeps and investigations.
