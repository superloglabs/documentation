title: "Dash0"

---

Superlog reads your Dash0 logs, metrics, and traces through your organization's Dash0 MCP server.

## Connect Dash0

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the MCP endpoint">
    In Dash0, open **Organization settings → Endpoints → MCP** and copy the MCP endpoint URL for your region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In Superlog, open **Integrations** and click **Add** on Dash0. Paste the **MCP endpoint URL**, click **Continue to Dash0**, and approve access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Superlog uses Dash0

- **Investigations.** Superlog queries Dash0 for evidence while it investigates a problem.
- **Tag mode** can read Dash0 when you turn it on in **Tag mode**.

Dash0 is not a source of new problems. To have Superlog find problems in the same data, also send it to Superlog with [OpenTelemetry](/guides/instrument-your-app).

## Access

Superlog uses only the Dash0 tools marked read-only. It blocks Agent0 delegation, so it does not use your Agent0 credits.
