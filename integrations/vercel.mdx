title: "Vercel"
description: "Connect Vercel so Superlog can read projects, deployments, and logs, and receive traces and logs through drains."
---

Superlog reads your Vercel projects, deployments, and logs. When you connect from setup, it also creates drains that send traces and logs to Superlog.

## What Superlog reads

- Projects, deployments, deployment checks, and domains.
- Logs.

Superlog does not request write access to deployments or access to environment variables, tokens, or secrets.

## Connect Vercel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Vercel page">
    Open **Setup → Sources**, click **Add source**, and choose **Vercel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the integration">
    Click **Continue to Vercel** and install the Superlog integration on your team. Share the projects Superlog should read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the drains">
    Back in Superlog, the page lists the drains under **Telemetry from**. A new drain shows **Waiting for data** until Vercel's first delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Integrations**, **Add** on Vercel installs the same integration without drains. To add drains later, see [Vercel telemetry](/collect-telemetry/vercel).

## How findings use Vercel

- **New errors.** Drains send your deployments' traces and logs to Superlog. Errors in them become error issues, and Superlog investigates new and regressed ones.
- **Investigations.** Superlog reads deployments and logs for evidence, for example to match an error to a deployment.

## Limits

- Drains need a Vercel Pro or Enterprise team.
- Drains cover the projects you share with the integration.
- Environment variables are not readable.
