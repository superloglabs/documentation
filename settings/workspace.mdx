A workspace holds your automations, integrations, secrets, runs, and members. Data and credentials are never shared across workspaces. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the account menu at the top of the sidebar.

Open **Settings** in the sidebar to manage the workspace. The tabs are **Workspace**, **Models**, and **Billing**.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything a member can do, plus invite people, change roles, and remove members. |
| **Member** | Create and edit automations, connect integrations, configure tag mode, and view runs. |

The person who creates a workspace is its first admin.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workspace tab">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Under **Invite a teammate**, enter their email, choose **Member** or **Admin**, and click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link if needed">
    Superlog emails the invitation. Click **Copy link** to share it another way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pending invitations are listed under **Pending invitations** with their expiry date. Admins can copy the link again or cancel the invitation.

## Manage members

The **Members** list shows everyone in the workspace. Admins can change a member's role or click **Remove** to take them out of the workspace.
