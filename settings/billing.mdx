Superlog plans differ by usage quota. Start free and move up as your automations grow. See current prices on the [pricing page](https://superlog.sh/pricing).

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 / month | Teams getting started. A light usage quota, no card required. |
| **Pro** | $100 / month | Teams running automations daily. |
| **Max** | $200 / month | Teams with the highest demand. |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Organizations that need a custom quota, terms, and support. [Talk to us](https://cal.com/superlog/yc). |

## What counts toward your quota

Two things:

- **AI inference** that runs on Superlog's included usage. Runs that use your own API key or ChatGPT subscription are billed by your provider and do not count here.
- **Sandbox compute**, the time each run's sandbox is running. This counts for every run, including runs on your own key.

Automation runs and tag mode questions both draw on the quota.

## Check your usage

Go to **Settings → Billing**. The **Included usage this month** card shows how much of the quota you have used, split into AI inference and sandbox compute, and when it resets.

## When you reach your quota

A banner appears in the app and Superlog sends a notice to Slack. Work that is already running finishes. New runs and tag mode questions do not start until the quota resets at the start of the next billing period or you upgrade.

## Change plans

In **Settings → Billing**, choose a plan:

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. Superlog charges the saved card for the prorated difference after you confirm.
- **Downgrades** and returning to Free take effect at the end of the current billing period.

Click **Manage billing** to update your payment method or see invoices.
