Superlog plans are priced by findings. Only findings posted to Slack count.

| Plan | Price | Seats | Findings | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pro** | $100 / month | 1 seat. Teammates need Team. | 50 included a month, then $2.50 per extra finding | Email |
| **Team** | $200 / month | Unlimited | 100 included a month, then $2.50 per extra finding | Email |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Custom limits | Support SLA |

Enterprise adds SSO and SAML, audit logs, and invoice billing. [Talk to us](https://cal.com/superlog/yc).

There is no free plan. Workspaces created on the earlier automations product keep their existing plan in **Settings → Billing**.

## What counts as a finding

A finding counts when Superlog posts it to your Slack channel. These do not count:

- Investigations and sweeps.
- Problems Superlog dismissed.
- Findings below your minimum severity, which stay on the **Findings** page.

Each finding counts once.

## Extra findings

Superlog keeps investigating and posting after you use your included findings. Each extra finding costs $2.50 on Pro and Team. Extra findings are billed at the end of the billing month. Included findings reset at the start of each billing month.

To limit how many investigations run in a day, set **Investigations per day** in [Finding settings](/findings/settings#general).

## Seats

Pro has one seat. To invite teammates, switch to Team. Team has unlimited seats, so everyone on your team can see findings and change settings.

## Telemetry storage

Superlog stores logs, traces, and metrics for 30 days. Every plan includes, each month:

| Data | Included | Beyond the included amount |
|---|---|---|
| Spans | 1M | $0.60 per million |
| Logs | 5M | $0.50 per million |
| Metric points | 10M | $0.20 per million |

You can also keep your telemetry in the tools you already use. Superlog reads it there.

## Check usage

Open **Setup → Plan**. It shows your plan, how many findings you used this month, any extra findings, and when the count resets. **Usage** in the sidebar shows findings per day.

## Change plans

In **Setup → Plan**:

- Click **Switch to Team** to move from Pro to Team at any time. On Team, **Switch to Pro** moves back.
- Click **Manage billing** to change the payment method, see invoices, or cancel the plan.

The first plan is chosen in the last step of [setup](/findings/quickstart). Superlog turns on when checkout finishes.
